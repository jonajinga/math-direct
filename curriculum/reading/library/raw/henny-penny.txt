# The Sky Is Falling

Henny Penny was a little brown hen with bright eyes. One day she was picking up corn in the yard. Something fell out of the tree and hit her on the head. "Oh my, the sky is falling," said Henny Penny. "I must go along the road and tell the king."

Soon she met Cocky Locky, the rooster. He asked, "Where are you going, Henny Penny?" "I am going to tell the king that the sky is falling," she said. Cocky Locky asked if he could come along too. "Of course you may," said Henny Penny with a nod. So the two of them went along the road together.

Next they met Ducky Lucky by the pond. Ducky Lucky asked, "Where are you two going?" "The sky is falling," said Henny Penny. "We are going to tell the king," said Cocky Locky. Ducky Lucky asked to come too, and they said yes. So the three of them went along the road together.

Then they met Goosey Loosey and Turkey Lurkey. The two of them asked, "Where are you all going?" "The sky is falling," said Henny Penny. "We are going to tell the king," said Ducky Lucky. They asked to come too, and Henny Penny said yes. So the five of them went along the road together.

# What the King Said

Before long the five friends met Foxy Loxy. The fox asked, "Where are you all going?" "The sky is falling," said Henny Penny. "We are going to tell the king," said the others. "The king's house is a long way off," said the fox. "I know a short cut through my den," he said. "Follow me, and we will be there in no time."

Henny Penny looked at the fox. She looked at his sharp teeth and his sly smile. "No thank you, we will keep to the road," said Henny Penny. The others nodded and stayed close to Henny Penny. At last the fox gave up and went home hungry.

The five friends walked on until they came to the king's house. The king came out to see what they wanted. "The sky is falling," said Henny Penny. "A piece of it fell and hit me on the head." The king looked at her head and smiled. "This is not a piece of the sky," he said. He picked something small and brown out of her feathers. "It is only an acorn," said the king. "It fell from the oak tree in your yard." Henny Penny looked at the acorn and felt very silly. The five friends laughed and went home for supper.

V: sly = clever in a tricky, hidden way
V: den = the home of a wild animal, often a hole
V: acorn = the small nut that grows on an oak tree
